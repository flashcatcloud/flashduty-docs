## Sampling and exact queries

With a large amount of data, not every chart on the analytics dashboards reads all of it. The **Sampled** mark next to the title means this chart was computed from a sample, so its values are estimates. Hover over the mark to see roughly what fraction of the data was sampled.

When you need the exact number for a chart, click the **Sampled** mark: that chart alone is computed once over all data, showing **Computing…** while it runs, and the mark disappears when it's done. Changing the time range or the filters puts the chart back on sampling.

An account can run a limited number of exact queries at once (2 by default), and the rest queue. A query that times out in the queue shows **Failed**; click the mark to retry.

A trend chart switched to **Cumulative** reads all data, so it shows no **Sampled** mark.

## Trend chart granularity and cumulative view

The **User Access Trend** chart on the overview page has two controls: the **Per interval / Cumulative** switch and the **Granularity** dropdown.

| Control | Values | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Trend view** | **Per interval** (default), **Cumulative** | Per interval is the value within each interval; cumulative is the running total from the start of the selected range to that interval |
| **Granularity** | **5 min**, **1 hour** (default), **1 day** | The time each point covers; when the current range cannot be drawn at that width, an available one is used instead |

- Every point in the cumulative view covers everything from the start of the range, so you can't drill down from one. UV counts each user once, so the end of the cumulative line is not the sum of per-interval UV.
- When a granularity cannot draw the current range, the dropdown says why: **Range too long** (too many points), **Range too short** (fewer than two points), or **Active devices are counted by the hour** (the line read from active devices can only be drawn hourly or coarser; see the next section).
- With **1 day**, each bucket starts at midnight in your display time zone (the one in [personal settings](/en/on-call/configuration/personal-settings), or the [organization time zone](/en/platform/organization-info) when you have not set your own), not at midnight UTC.

## Clicking charts, fullscreen, and comparison with the previous period

Data points on most chart cards are clickable: points on a time-series chart, bars, pie slices, and ranking bars all open an action menu. The items depend on the card. In Overview, **User Access Trend** offers **View Session Details** (the session list) and **See View details**, **Page Error Rate** offers **View Error Pages**, and **API Success Rate** offers **View Error Requests**. Performance cards offer **Views: View Details** and **Vitals: View Details**, and resource cards offer **Resource: View Details**.

Selecting an item opens the [Viewer](/en/rum/explorer/overview) in a new tab as a list of the matching events: the query carries the dashboard's filters and the clicked point's condition, and for a time-series point the time range narrows to that point's bucket. Every card with a drill-down menu works this way. A trend chart switched to **Cumulative** has no such menu; see the previous section for why.

The expand icon in a card's top-right corner, shown when you hover over the card or focus it from the keyboard, opens a fullscreen dialog. The dialog has its own filter bar and time range picker, and changing them re-runs only this chart without touching the dashboard. Clicking a point inside the dialog opens the Viewer on the dialog's filters and range. Not every card has the icon: the pie cards on the Errors tab and the cards on the Mini Program Performance tab, for example, do not.

To the right of a card's value you see the difference against the previous period with an up or down icon. Hover over it to see the two windows being compared: the current range shifted back by a window of the same length. Green means the metric moved in the good direction, red means it moved the wrong way. Nothing is shown when the previous period was 0, equals the current value, or has no data.

## Active devices and excluding crawlers

The **Active Devices** card on the overview page counts the devices that reported or opened a page in the selected range, deduplicated by the SDK's anonymous ID. On a web application, that card also carries an **Exclude crawlers** switch in its top right, on by default.

| Switch | Devices counted |
|--------|-----------------|
| **On** (default) | Devices judged crawlers leave the count: a user agent that declares a crawler or a headless browser (for example Baiduspider, Googlebot, Bytespider, or HeadlessChrome), a browser reporting that automation drives it, and a visit whose user agent contradicts its browser brand information all stay out |
| Off | Every device that reported counts, crawlers included |

The switch moves only this card's number and the device trend line beside it; **Logged-in Users** is unaffected. It is not saved with the page, so a reload returns it to on.

Active devices are recorded by the hour, so the device trend line can be drawn at **1 hour** or coarser only: picking **5 min** leaves that option disabled and labelled **Active devices are counted by the hour**.

## Ask AI on a card

Every headline card on the analytics dashboards has an **Ask AI** button (a sparkle icon) in its top-right corner, shown when you hover over the card or focus it from the keyboard. It opens the AI SRE panel with a question about that card: the question carries the card's title, the value on the card, and the current application, tab, filters, and time range, so the answer quotes the numbers you see.

When the card's value moved 20% or more in the wrong direction against the previous period, the button stays visible and shows **Moved notably, ask AI why**.

These cards have no **Ask AI** button:

- The selected time range is longer than 14 days.
- AI SRE is not enabled for the account, or you have no AI SRE chat access.
- The card has no value, or its reading failed.

If the account has no enabled **RUM** skill, selecting the button first shows **Install the RUM skill to ask AI**; select **Install** to open the skill catalog. Once it is installed, return to the dashboard and ask.
