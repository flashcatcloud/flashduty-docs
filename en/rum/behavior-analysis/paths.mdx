Paths centre on one page and show the pages sessions visited immediately before and after it, answering how users arrived at a page and where they went when they left.

## Reading the flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the centre page">
    **Centre page** is either **Session entry page** (the session's first page) or any page. Only the session's first arrival at the centre page is counted, and consecutive visits to the same page are shown once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a direction">
    **Following paths** follows the pages visited after the centre page; **Preceding paths** follows the ones visited before it. There is no preceding path when the centre page is the session entry page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the depth">
    **Depth** is 2, 3, 4 or 5, and defaults to 3. Each level shows the five pages with the most sessions; the rest fold into "Other".
  </Step>
</Steps>

Filters match the funnel's: **Environment**, **Version**, **Device type**, **Browser**, **Operating system** and **Country**.

## Reading the result

- **Top entry pages**, **Top exits** and **Top sources**: the leading pages on each side of the centre page, with their session counts.
- **Flow chart**: page nodes and links by level, each node carrying its session count and share.
- **Frequent paths**: the most common complete page sequences, listed separately.
- **Example sessions**: up to 20 sessions that took the path, each with a copyable session ID or a link to open it in the explorer.

On a large number of sessions the page samples by session and states the share the estimate is based on; counts are then converted back to the whole population, so the proportions hold while the absolute numbers are estimates.

## From a path to a funnel

Select a link, a node or a frequent path to **build a funnel from A → B**, which opens [Funnels](/en/rum/behavior-analysis/funnel) with those two pages as the first steps. You can also re-centre on a page as **Set as entry page** or **Centre on this page** and keep exploring.

## When there's no data

**No data** usually means the centre page had no views in the time range, or the filters are too narrow. Try another centre page, loosen the filters, or widen the time range.
