> AI SRE must be activated in the console, is available on On-call Pro or higher, and is billed on actual usage. See [Activation and billing](/en/ai-sre/overview#activation-and-billing).

## What context is

Context is the material AI SRE can reuse across troubleshooting sessions. It has two parts, each with its own page under the **Context** group of the **AI SRE → Customize** sidebar.

- **[Knowledge](/en/ai-sre/knowledge)** is maintained by people. It describes how this environment runs: a guide named `DUTY.md`, plus runbooks, service lists, and similar files. A session reads the guide first and opens the other files when it needs them. Entry: **AI SRE → Customize → Context → Knowledge**.
- **[Memory](/en/ai-sre/memory)** is organized by the system. After a session, it keeps preferences, procedures, facts, and lessons that will still matter later. You can also ask, in the conversation, to remember or forget something. Entry: **AI SRE → Customize → Context → Memory**.

Both pages have an input box at the bottom: write what to change, and the console opens a new chat where the AI first explains the change and saves it after you agree.

## Three scopes

Both kinds of material are organized by scope, but they support different scopes.

| Scope | Memory | Knowledge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | Yours | None |
| Team | That team's | That team's (at most one per team) |
| Shared | None | The whole account's |

Personal scope has no knowledge. Shared scope has no memory.
