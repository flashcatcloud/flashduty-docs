## What context is

Context is the material AI SRE can reuse across troubleshooting sessions. It has two parts, each with its own page under the **Context** group of the **AI SRE → Customize** sidebar.

- **[Knowledge](/en/ai-sre/knowledge)** is maintained by people. It describes how this environment runs: a guide named `DUTY.md`, plus runbooks, service lists, and similar files. A session reads the guide first and opens the other files when it needs them. Entry: **AI SRE → Customize → Context → Knowledge**.
- **[Memory](/en/ai-sre/memory)** is organized by the system. After a session, it keeps preferences, procedures, facts, and lessons that will still matter later. You can also ask, in the conversation, to remember or forget something. Entry: **AI SRE → Customize → Context → Memory**.

Both pages have an input box at the bottom. Describe what to change, and the console opens a new chat. The AI explains the change first and saves it after you agree.

## Three scopes

Both kinds of material are organized by scope, but they support different scopes.

| Scope | Memory | Knowledge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal | Yours | None |
| Team | That team's | That team's (at most one per team) |
| Shared | None | The whole account's |

Personal scope has no knowledge. Shared scope has no memory.

<span id="scope-and-permissions"></span>
## Scope and permissions

Everything under Customize (skills, knowledge, MCP, agents, environments) uses the same two-level scope: **shared** (account level) and **team**. The account is the security boundary. A team only marks ownership and edit rights.

| Action | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Use | Any account member can use the account's resources in a session. |
| Create | Creating in a team requires membership in that team, including for account owners and admins. Creating in shared scope is limited to account owners and admins. |
| Edit, delete | Team resources can be changed only by members of that team. Account owners and admins must join the team first. Shared resources are limited to account owners and admins. The creator has no extra rights. |
| Change ownership | Account owners and admins can move a resource to any team. Other members can move it only to a team they belong to. Setting a resource to shared is limited to account owners and admins. |
| Loaded in a session | A session bound to a team loads shared resources and that team's resources only, including for account owners and admins. A personal session loads shared resources and the resources of the teams the session creator belongs to. |
| List | Management pages show shared resources and resources of your teams. |

For troubleshooting that spans teams, ask the other team to set the relevant resources to shared.
