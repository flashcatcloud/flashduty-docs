## Overview

---

Incidents usually unfold right inside your IM channel — alerts come in, the team discusses, someone spins up a war room. AI SRE puts the agent directly into that collaboration flow: **you never have to switch to the console to summon it**, and your teammates can follow its analysis in real time.

AI SRE's IM integration has two trigger modes:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="@mention Summon" icon="at">
    **@mention AI SRE** in a group chat or DM and describe the problem to start or continue an investigation. It replies **in-thread**, and the conversation context is tied to that IM session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="War Room Auto-Diagnosis" icon="people-group">
    When an IM war room is opened for an incident, AI SRE **automatically runs an initial diagnosis** and posts the findings as an analysis message in the war room — no one needs to manually summon it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Supported IM Platforms

---

AI SRE's IM interaction covers four major platforms. Each supports inbound @mentions (via webhook), history message retrieval, and outbound replies:

| Platform | Group Chat @mention | Direct Message (DM) | War Room Auto-Diagnosis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slack | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Lark | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| DingTalk | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| WeCom | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

<Note>
IM interaction requires that you have already connected the corresponding platform's bot in Flashduty (the same IM bot used for alert notifications and collaboration). Complete the bot configuration in Flashduty's IM integration first; only then can AI SRE send and receive messages on that platform.
</Note>

## @mention Summon

---

In a connected IM channel, **@mention the bot** and write your question (for example, "@AI SRE check why the payment service is seeing a spike in 5xx errors"). The message is forwarded to AI SRE via the platform webhook:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detect the Mention">
    The platform distinguishes between **group chat @mentions** and **direct messages**. Once mentioned, AI SRE deduplicates the event and checks whether the message contains an imperative instruction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind to a Session">
    AI SRE uses "account + platform + chat" as the key to locate a session. Multiple @mentions within the same IM chat are routed to the **same agent session**, preserving context. For a new chat, the platform includes the thread's starting message and the associated incident (if any) as initial context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Context and Reply">
    AI SRE reads the thread or chat history to build context, investigates autonomously, and posts its findings **in-thread**. Other users mentioned alongside AI SRE in the original message are also carried through and included in the reply, making multi-party collaboration in the channel seamless.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The **reply mode** is configurable (off / first / all), controlling whether AI SRE @mentions the asker in its reply and whether it replies **in-thread** or in the main channel. In a busy, high-volume channel, in-thread replies keep the investigation discussion focused without flooding the main feed.
</Tip>

## War Room Auto-Diagnosis

---

When you open a **war room** for an incident in IM, AI SRE intervenes **automatically** — no one needs to @mention it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the War Room">
    Create the war room (Lark / DingTalk / WeCom / Slack group) through the incident's collaboration flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trigger Diagnosis in the Background">
    Once the war room is created, the platform triggers an initial diagnosis as a **non-blocking background task**, launching AI SRE with the incident's context loaded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post Findings">
    When the diagnosis is complete, AI SRE posts the analysis as a message back in the war room. By the time the team starts investigating, the first-pass analysis is already there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
War room auto-diagnosis is bound to the incident context: when the investigation begins, the corresponding `incident_id` is attached to the run so AI SRE can read the incident details, timeline, and recent changes. For details on incident / war room integration with A2A, see [A2A Agents · Incident and War Room Integration](/en/ai-sre/agents#incident-and-war-room-integration).
</Note>

## Relationship to Console Sessions

---

Whether initiated from IM or the console, the underlying runtime is the **same session engine**: a single actor driving execution serially, streaming output, context compaction, and optional team and environment bindings. The only difference is the entry point —

- **Console**: Ask questions one by one in the Chat workspace and view the full tool-call and sub-session panels.
- **IM**: @mention it from your everyday collaboration channel; findings are posted in-thread. This is ideal for getting quick analysis at the incident scene before diving deeper in the console.

For details on the session runtime, see [Chat & Sessions](/en/ai-sre/sessions).
