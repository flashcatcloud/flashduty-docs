The **cloud sandbox** is a temporary execution environment managed by Flashduty: an isolated, ready-to-use container. The agent's tool calls (running commands, reading and writing files, running Skills, connecting to MCP) all happen inside it, and you don't install or maintain anything.

When no [self-hosted Runner](/en/ai-sre/environments#byoc-runner) is online and usable, sessions use the cloud sandbox by default. You can also pin a session to it manually. For the selection order and the per-session lock, see [Environments](/en/ai-sre/environments#selecting-an-environment-in-a-session).

## Location and lifecycle

- **Location**: the sandbox runs on the hosting provider's serverless platform, in Hong Kong by default.
- **Idle pause**: the sandbox pauses automatically when the session is idle and resumes on your next message, continuing in the same working directory.
- **Temporary**: the sandbox is not a persistent machine. Don't leave data in it that you need to keep.

## Egress

The sandbox can reach any public address and **cannot reach your private network**: your VPC, dedicated lines, and the databases, APIs, and jump hosts behind them are unreachable. When an investigation needs to connect to those targets, use a [self-hosted Runner](/en/ai-sre/environments#byoc-runner) on a machine that can reach your internal systems.

The console's network access option is "Allow all", set in a [cloud environment template](/en/ai-sre/environments#cloud-environment-templates).

## Cloud sandbox vs. self-hosted Runner

| Dimension | Cloud sandbox | Self-hosted Runner |
|---|---|---|
| Deployment | No install, system-managed | Deploy a persistent process on your own machine |
| Private-network reach | Not reachable; public addresses only | Can reach your VPC, intranet, and dedicated lines |
| Data residency | Inside the managed environment | Stays within your own network boundary |
| Lifecycle | Temporary, auto-pauses when idle | Persistent, under your control |
| Best for | Quick start, demos, public diagnostics | Deep investigations that reach real production |
