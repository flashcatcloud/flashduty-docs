<Info>
  **Public beta**: AI SRE is now open to all accounts. Accounts that have not activated it run in trial mode (usage is recorded but not billed); once you activate it (or switch a trial account to paid billing), usage is billed in credits. Features and the UI may continue to evolve.
</Info>

AI SRE is metered in **credits**. Accounts that have not activated it run in **trial mode**: usage is recorded but not billed. Once you enable AI SRE (or switch a trial-mode account to paid billing), usage is deducted in this order: **trial credits → credit packages → pay-as-you-go**.

This page covers the billing entry points, activation and purchasing, unit rates, low-credit reminders, and what happens when your credits run out. The complete package terms (validity, deduction order, refunds, and more) are in the [AI SRE credit package purchase agreement](/en/compliance/ai-sre-credit-package-purchase-agreement).

## Where to manage billing

---

In the console, open **Payment → Plan** from the left navigation and select the **AI SRE** tab. From there you can:

| Action | Location |
| --- | --- |
| Enable AI SRE / switch to paid billing | The **Enable AI SRE** or **Switch to paid billing** button on the tab |
| Check your credit balance | The **Credit balance** card: trial credits remaining, package credits remaining, and the earliest expiry date |
| Check and set pay-as-you-go | The **Pay-as-you-go** card: spend this period and the cap setting |
| Buy credit packages | The **Buy credit packages** button, which opens `/wallet/plan/ai-sre/purchase` |

When your account is blocked, the banner above the chat composer also offers **Go to AI SRE billing**, which opens the AI SRE tab of the Plan page directly.

## Activation and prerequisites

---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the AI SRE tab">
    Go to **Payment → Plan → AI SRE** in the console.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the activation button">
    An account that has not enabled AI SRE clicks **Enable AI SRE**; an account recorded in trial mode during the public beta clicks **Switch to paid billing**. Both place a ¥0 order — activation itself is free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once the order completes, AI SRE is enabled and pay-as-you-go is available by default. You can set a cap on the **Pay-as-you-go** card at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Prerequisites:

- The account must hold a **Professional (or higher) On-Call plan**. Below Professional, AI SRE activation and credit-package enquiries and orders are refused with an upgrade prompt; settlement, renewal, and reward orders are not affected.
- The activation and purchase buttons require the **Payment Manage** permission (the activation button is labeled *Wallet purchase permission required*); viewing the AI SRE tab requires the **Payment Read** permission. Changing the pay-as-you-go cap also requires account admin.
- If the wallet balance is negative, activation is refused and the page asks you to top up first.

## Billing modes

---

- **Trial mode (not activated)**: usage is recorded but not billed, and it is not billed retroactively after you activate. The console shows *Trial mode: usage is not charged*.
- **Switch to paid billing**: a trial-mode account can convert on its own (also a ¥0 order), after which credit packages and pay-as-you-go become available.
- **Paid billing**: billing starts the moment you activate. Usage is then deducted in this order: **trial credits → credit packages (earliest expiring first) → pay-as-you-go**.

<Note>
Trial credits are granted by Flashduty per billing period: at most one grant of **30 credits** per period, and only to accounts that are activated, not in trial mode, hold no usable credit packages, and spent nothing on pay-as-you-go in the previous period. They expire with the period and do not carry over. If AI SRE service is terminated for the account, any unused trial credits are cleared; the balance and validity of purchased packages are unaffected.
</Note>

## Credit packages

---

A credit package is a one-time, account-wide purchase of prepaid credits. It is not a subscription and does not renew automatically. The fixed tiers are:

| Package | Credits | Price |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1,000 credits | 1,000 | ¥950 |
| 5,000 credits | 5,000 | ¥4,500 |
| 20,000 credits | 20,000 | ¥17,000 |

- Each order buys one package; multipliers within a single order are not supported.
- A package is valid for **365 days** from the moment payment succeeds. Credits still unused at expiry are lost.
- An account can hold multiple packages at once (same or different tiers). Credits accumulate, and the package expiring soonest is drawn down first.
- Packages do not renew or top up automatically.
- Credits can only be used against AI SRE usage. They cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred to another account, or applied to other Flashduty products.

<Note>
Buying a package does not enable AI SRE. Enable it first — a package's validity starts when payment succeeds, and an account that has not enabled AI SRE consumes no credits.
</Note>

To buy a package:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a package">
    Open the purchase page (`/wallet/plan/ai-sre/purchase`) and choose a tier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the agreement">
    Select the *AI SRE credit package purchase agreement* checkbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Buy now**. If your balance is short you can top up first; overseas accounts with Stripe enabled can also pay the difference by card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pay-as-you-go

---

Pay-as-you-go is settled from the account's general wallet at **1 credit = ¥1**. On the **Pay-as-you-go** card you can set one of three states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Unlimited** (default) | Pay-as-you-go continues after packages run out, with no cap |
| **Set a monthly cap** | Enter a cap in yuan (¥); pay-as-you-go spend for the period cannot exceed it |
| **Forbid pay-as-you-go** | Pay-as-you-go is never used; AI SRE stops running once packages are exhausted |

Once the cap is reached, new runs stop; the run already in progress can finish. Actual pay-as-you-go charges never exceed the cap you set.

## Billing period

---

The AI SRE billing period follows your account's **On-Call subscription cycle (its license window)** — it is not a calendar month. Credits and pay-as-you-go are both settled per that period: trial credits expire with the period, and pay-as-you-go spend resets at the start of each period (the cap you set stays in place). The exact dates are shown as **Current period** on the AI SRE tab.

## Unit rates

---

AI SRE charges credits against three kinds of usage:

| Item | Rate (credits) |
| --- | ---: |
| Model usage · fresh input tokens | 8 per million tokens |
| Model usage · cached input tokens | 0.16 per million tokens |
| Model usage · output tokens | 32 per million tokens |
| Sandbox runtime (billed per **online minute**: an online sandbox is charged one full minute, and part of a minute counts as a minute) | 2 per online hour |
| Web search (successful search requests) | 0.25 per request |

These are the current rates (rate version 2026-09-10-v4). **Rates may change; the price list published at the time of use applies.** A rate change is never applied retroactively to usage already charged. Model usage is priced by model tier, so a provider or failover switch on our side never changes what you pay.

## Low-credit reminders

---

When the credits available for the period (trial credits + packages + pay-as-you-go cap, combined) drop to **20%**, Flashduty sends an **SMS and email** reminder to the account and its enabled admins:

- Credits expiring within 7 days count as already spent, so an approaching expiry also triggers the reminder.
- The reminder is sent once per period at a given credit level; if you buy a package and the account later approaches its limit again, another reminder is sent.
- Accounts in trial mode (not billed) never receive it, and neither do accounts whose pay-as-you-go is set to **Unlimited** — they have no point at which credits run out.

## When your credits run out

---

- **In the console**: a persistent banner appears above the composer (it cannot be dismissed), the Send button is disabled and shows the reason, and the banner's **Go to AI SRE billing** link opens the AI SRE tab of the Plan page.
- **On the server**: no new runs are accepted. A run that has already started is not interrupted and can finish. Anything beyond the credits and authorized pay-as-you-go available at that moment is covered by Flashduty and is not charged to you.

There are three reasons an account can be blocked, each with its own remedy:

| Reason | Console message | How to unblock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI SRE not enabled | AI SRE is not enabled for this account. Enable it to continue. | Enable AI SRE on the AI SRE tab |
| Period allowance exhausted | This account has used its full AI SRE credits allowance for this period. Buy a credit package or raise the usage limit to continue. | Buy a package, raise the pay-as-you-go cap, or wait for the next period |
| Wallet balance insufficient for pay-as-you-go | The account balance is insufficient. Top up the wallet to continue using AI SRE on pay-as-you-go; a recent top-up may take a few minutes to take effect. | Top up the account wallet |

## Refunds and arrears

---

- **Refunds**: there is no self-service refund for packages, refunds are handled per whole package, and partial refunds are not supported. A package can be refunded only if it is **completely unused** and **not expired** — contact [support@flashcat.cloud](mailto:support@flashcat.cloud) to apply. See the [AI SRE credit package purchase agreement](/en/compliance/ai-sre-credit-package-purchase-agreement#refunds) for the full terms.
- **Account arrears**: if the wallet is exhausted or the account falls into arrears, any new run that would have to be funded by pay-as-you-go is refused. Trial credits and purchased packages are prepaid and are unaffected — while the account still has usable trial or package credits, runs can still be started, and a package's balance and validity are unchanged. Once the account is topped up, nothing needs to be re-enabled by hand: the ~30-minute sweep restores pay-as-you-go automatically (the wallet balance cache may take a few minutes to refresh too).

For topping up, invoicing, and other wallet capabilities (including the separate *low balance reminder* for your wallet balance), see [Pricing · Payment and top-up](/en/platform/pricing#payment-and-top-up).
