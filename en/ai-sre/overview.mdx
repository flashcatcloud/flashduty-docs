## What is AI SRE

AI SRE is Flashduty's autonomous SRE agent platform. You give the AI instructions through conversation. It investigates incidents, diagnoses root causes, and calls tools to run diagnostics on its own. It also saves the operational knowledge from each investigation for later reuse.

It plans its own steps, reads and writes files, queries monitoring and logs, runs commands, calls external tools (MCP), and delegates subtasks when needed. Its conclusions come with the investigation trail.

AI SRE is deeply integrated with Flashduty's incident response system. When Flashduty generates an incident or opens a war room, you can directly trigger an AI SRE session so the agent starts with the incident context. You can collaborate in the console or by **@ mentioning it directly in your IM group (Slack / Feishu / DingTalk / WeCom)**.

## Typical use cases

AI SRE has an entry point from incident trigger to retrospective:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Conversational Troubleshooting" icon="comments">
  Ask questions proactively in the console's Chat workspace: why a service is behaving abnormally, the root cause of an alert, or the blast radius of a change. The agent streams its planning, tool calls, and intermediate findings before delivering a final conclusion.
</Card>
<Card title="On-Demand in IM" icon="comment-dots">
  No context switching needed — in Slack, Feishu, DingTalk, or WeCom group chats or direct messages, **@ AI SRE** to start or continue an investigation. It replies **in-thread** so all team members stay in the loop. See [IM Integration](/en/ai-sre/im).
</Card>
<Card title="Automatic War Room Diagnosis" icon="people-group">
  When someone opens a war room for an incident and an enabled [automation rule](/en/ai-sre/automations) with the **War room created** trigger matches it, AI SRE **automatically runs an initial diagnostic** and posts the conclusion to the war room. A first-pass analysis is already in the channel when your team starts investigating.
</Card>
<Card title="Usage Insights" icon="gauge-high">
  Use [`/insight`](/en/ai-sre/insight) to review the past 30 days of sessions, quantify where you're spending time, identify missing runbooks and repeatedly copy-pasted context, and receive actionable improvement suggestions.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Activation and billing

AI SRE is available on every plan. Activate it separately in the console before use; charges are based on actual usage. It does not require an On-call subscription or occupy a member license. Members still need the appropriate permissions.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Billing" icon="gift">
  AI SRE is settled in credits (1 credit = ¥1). Metered items are model usage, sandbox online time, and web search. Activation is free, and eligible accounts get gift credits each billing period. You can also buy credit packages, valid 365 days from payment (sizes and prices are in the purchase agreement). Pay-as-you-go is off by default; an admin can turn it on and optionally set a per-period cap. Full terms: [AI SRE Credit Package Purchase Agreement](/en/compliance/ai-sre-credit-package-purchase-agreement).

  When gift credits, credit packages, and the wallet balance are all used up, AI SRE pauses new conversations. It shows the reason above the composer, with a **Go to AI SRE billing** button. An admin can turn on pay-as-you-go, buy a credit package or top up there, and you can then continue.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Human Authorization" icon="user-check">
  Production changes, restarts, rollbacks, and external notifications all require your confirmation before they execute.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Activating and closing AI SRE

In the console, open **Payment → Plan** and select the **AI SRE** tab to activate or close AI SRE yourself. This is available on SaaS only and requires the **Payment Read** and wallet purchase permissions.

| Action | Cost | Result |
|------|------|------|
| **Enable AI SRE** | Free | AI SRE is available right away |
| **Disable AI SRE** | No charge and no refund | The account can no longer start new investigations; a run already in progress is not interrupted and its usage is still metered |

After closing:

- Gift credits and purchased credit packages **keep** their balances and validity, and you can use them again when you re-activate. Gift credits are still valid only in the billing period they were granted for.
- Credit package refunds follow the [AI SRE Credits Package Purchase Agreement](/en/compliance/ai-sre-credit-package-purchase-agreement).
- You can close AI SRE even with an overdue balance or a lapsed subscription.
- If closing fails, retry.

### Independent billing periods and IM applications

Billing and gift-credit periods run in consecutive 30-day windows from the first AI SRE activation. Disabling and re-enabling the service does not reset the period or grant that period’s gift credits again. Existing accounts retain their current period and continue with independent 30-day periods when it ends.

IM applications can be created, authorized, and enabled on every plan for AI SRE direct or group conversations. Using an application as an On-call notification target, including escalation policies and on-call reminders, still requires an active On-call Professional plan. Otherwise, selection is disabled, the server rejects the configuration, and restricted notifications from existing configurations are not sent. On-call expiry or downgrade does not interrupt AI SRE conversations.

On-call actions such as acknowledging or resolving an incident retain their existing permission and license requirements. AI SRE analysis does not grant additional incident-management permissions.

## Core capabilities

You can configure and manage all of the following in the console.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Conversational Troubleshooting" icon="comments" href="/en/ai-sre/sessions">
  Collaborate with the agent session by session. A session handles your messages one at a time, with streaming output, cancellation at any time, automatic compaction for long conversations, and one-click launch from an incident.
</Card>
<Card title="IM Platform" icon="comment-dots" href="/en/ai-sre/im">
  @ the agent in Slack / Feishu / DingTalk / WeCom to start or continue an investigation, and receive rule-driven initial diagnostics in incident war rooms.
</Card>
<Card title="Skills" icon="wrench" href="/en/ai-sre/skills">
  Skill packages that the agent can invoke, encapsulating reusable troubleshooting workflows. Scope can be set to Shared (account-level) or Team; once enabled, they are loaded on demand during sessions.
</Card>
<Card title="Manage Knowledge" icon="book" href="/en/ai-sre/knowledge">
  Knowledge indexed through DUTY.md as the entry point and @-reference links, carrying service catalogs, runbooks, on-call routing, and other long-lived context — loaded in layers by account and team.
</Card>
<Card title="MCP (External Tools)" icon="plug" href="/en/ai-sre/mcp">
  Connect external tools and data sources through the Model Context Protocol.
</Card>
<Card title="Agent" icon="satellite-dish" href="/en/ai-sre/agents">
  Delegate tasks to external remote agents through the standard A2A protocol. AI SRE also exposes its own Agent Card so external clients can invoke it in reverse.
</Card>
<Card title="Environments" icon="server" href="/en/ai-sre/environments">
  Where the agent runs commands: Flashduty-managed cloud sandboxes by default, or a self-hosted Runner on your own machine so investigations can reach your private network.
</Card>
<Card title="Usage Insights" icon="gauge-high" href="/en/ai-sre/insight">
  Use /insight to review the past 30 days of AI SRE sessions and receive a quantified overview, narrative summary, and actionable operational improvement suggestions (read-only; nothing is applied automatically).
</Card>
<Card title="Automations" icon="robot" href="/en/ai-sre/automations">
  Have AI SRE run a hidden session on a cron schedule, through an API call, or on an On-call incident to automatically produce periodic inspections, insights, or post-mortems.
</Card>
<Card title="Apps" icon="grid-2" href="/en/ai-sre/apps">
  Authorize GitHub, GitLab, or a Kubernetes cluster so AI SRE can work in your code repositories and clusters. It can understand code, investigate PRs / MRs and commits, open a PR or file an issue on request, or act within the scope you set on a cluster.
</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Console navigation

After entering AI SRE, the left sidebar has four entries (menu names match the console):

| Entry | Purpose |
|------|------|
| New chat | The primary workspace for collaborating with the agent on troubleshooting. The sidebar lists your sessions (with search, filtering, pinning, and archiving); the right side shows the conversation and investigation trail. See [Chat](/en/ai-sre/sessions). |
| Automations | Background tasks triggered on a schedule, by an API call, or by an On-call incident event. See [Automations](/en/ai-sre/automations). |
| Artifacts | View and manage the files and reports the agent publishes: search, and filter by personal / team scope. Each artifact can be copy-linked, downloaded, renamed, or deleted (renaming and deletion require edit permission). See [Artifacts](/en/ai-sre/artifacts). |
| Customize | Opens a second-level sidebar for the material, extensions, and execution environments the agent uses, in three groups (see the table below). Clicking it opens **Plugins → Overview** by default. |

The three groups under **Customize**:

| Group | Pages | Purpose |
|------|------|------|
| Context | Knowledge, Memory | [Knowledge](/en/ai-sre/knowledge): one Shared knowledge for the whole account, and at most one team knowledge per team. [Memory](/en/ai-sre/memory): preferences, procedures, facts, and lessons the AI notes in conversations, split into personal and team. |
| Plugins | Overview, Apps, Skill, MCP, Agents | **Overview** is the plugin catalog (MCP templates, Skill templates, built-in Apps); [Apps](/en/ai-sre/apps) (authorized external applications, for example, GitHub and GitLab), [Skill](/en/ai-sre/skills), [MCP](/en/ai-sre/mcp) (external tools), [Agents](/en/ai-sre/agents) (A2A remote agents). |
| Environments | Self-hosted, Cloud | [Self-hosted](/en/ai-sre/environments): Runners deployed on your machines; **Cloud**: cloud sandbox templates. When no self-hosted Runner is available, sessions use the cloud sandbox. |

<Note>
Menus or sub-tabs you do not have permission for are not shown.
</Note>
