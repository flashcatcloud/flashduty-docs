## Scope

AI SRE is metered in credits. A credit package is a one-time, account-scoped purchase of credits. It is not a subscription, seat, or auto-renewing service. You can purchase a package while AI SRE is closed. To use AI SRE, however, your account must have AI SRE enabled, an active On-call Pro or higher subscription, and an available AI SRE License for the person using it.

Packages are shared by the account and are not affected by the number of seats. Purchasing a package does not enable AI SRE and does not replace the subscription or authorization requirements for On-call or AI SRE Licenses.

## Credit deduction and pay-as-you-go billing

For each AI SRE usage event, credits are deducted in this order:

1. Available gifted credits for the current period;
2. Purchased package credits that expire first; then
3. Pay-as-you-go (PAYG).

Your account may receive a 10-credit gift when it meets the current-period conditions: it has no usable purchased package and had no PAYG usage in the preceding period. The first period follows the same rule. Gifted credits are available only in that period, do not roll over or transfer, and are not refundable.

Enabling AI SRE authorizes PAYG. PAYG is settled from your account’s general wallet at `1 credit = ¥1`. You can optionally set a 30-day-period cap on the AI SRE billing page. If you do not set a cap, or clear it, PAYG is unlimited. The period follows the current On-call billing period: it is a fixed consecutive 30 days in the account’s current On-call billing timezone.

When gifted credits, purchased packages, and authorized PAYG are all insufficient, the system stops admitting new AI SRE runs. One top-level run that has already started may finish, and its subsequent valid model usage remains billable. As a result, the final usage can exceed the remaining credits or PAYG cap shown at that moment.

## Billing rules

Account billing pages show credits only, not model-token detail. AI SRE records the usage facts, while Flashduty’s account settlement system produces settlement and bills.

Internal settlement amounts and credits are precise to five decimal places. Account pages round displayed amounts and credits to two decimal places. Display precision does not change the settled result.

- A model call with valid token usage is charged for its model-token usage, whether the task ultimately succeeds, fails, or is stopped.
- A failed retrieval request is not charged for retrieval.
- An abnormal, invalid, or unowned sandbox interval is not charged for sandbox usage.
- AI-generated output is for reference only. You are responsible for validating it against your own business, permissions, and change-management process, and for decisions to act or not act on it.

## Refunds

There is no self-service package-refund flow. To request a refund, contact [support@flashcat.cloud](mailto:support@flashcat.cloud) with your account, order ID, and reason for the request.

Flashduty reviews refund eligibility manually. Only unused, unexpired purchased package credits that are not frozen by a refund process can be considered. If the account has a top-level AI SRE run in progress, the refund is processed after the run ends. An approved refund equals the original order’s actual transaction unit price multiplied by its remaining refundable credits, and is returned to the account’s general wallet using that order’s original cash/bonus payment composition; it is not a full-order refund. Gifted credits are not refundable.

Whether a refund is approved and its amount are subject to the review result, applicable law, and any separate agreement between you and Flashduty. Nothing in this Agreement limits any refund right that cannot be excluded by law.
