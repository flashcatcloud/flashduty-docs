## Scope

AI SRE is metered in credits. A credit package is a one-time, account-scoped purchase of credits. It is not a subscription, seat, or auto-renewing service. You can purchase a package while AI SRE is closed. Using AI SRE, however, requires all three of the following:

1. AI SRE is enabled for the account;
2. The account holds an active On-call Pro or higher subscription;
3. The member starting the work occupies an available Flashduty license (drawn from the same license pool as On-call and other products — there is no separately sold AI SRE license).

Packages are shared by the account and are not affected by the number of seats. Purchasing a package does not enable AI SRE and does not replace the subscription and license requirements above.

<Warning>
Enable AI SRE before you buy a package. A package's validity period starts at successful payment, and an account without AI SRE enabled consumes no credits at all — buy first and enable later, and the package will sit idle while its validity runs out.
</Warning>

Confirm the package and price before you purchase. Package credits can only offset AI SRE usage; they cannot be redeemed for cash, transferred to another account, or used for other Flashduty products.
</Note>

## Credit deduction and pay-as-you-go billing

For each AI SRE usage event, credits are deducted in this order:

1. Any gifted credits Flashduty has granted to the account, if any;
2. Purchased package credits that expire first — when several packages are held, the one expiring soonest is drawn down first; then
3. Pay-as-you-go (PAYG).

Gifted credits (shown as “trial credits” in the console) are unrelated to this purchase and are granted automatically under the following rules: **at most one grant per billing period, 30 credits each**, and only to accounts that meet all of these conditions — AI SRE is enabled, the account is not in trial mode, it holds no usable credit package, and the previous billing period produced no pay-as-you-go usage. Gifted credits expire at the end of the billing period they were granted for, do not carry over between periods, and cannot be transferred or refunded; any unused gifted credits are zeroed out when AI SRE is terminated for the account, while purchased-package balances and validity are unaffected. An account holding a usable package, or one that produced pay-as-you-go usage in the previous billing period, receives no gift for the current period.

Enabling AI SRE authorizes PAYG. PAYG is settled from your account's general wallet at `1 credit = ¥1`. The billing period follows the account's **On-call subscription cycle** — it is the account's On-call license billing window (rolling from the subscription's start date by the subscription's own period length, **not a calendar month**): the “Current period” shown on the AI SRE billing page is exactly that window, and its help text reads “The billing period follows your On-Call subscription cycle; credits and pay-as-you-go are both settled per this period.” The window's start and end are displayed in the account's timezone. Gifted credits expire when that window ends, and the pay-as-you-go amount used resets with the window (a new period starts from zero).

On the AI SRE billing page you can put PAYG into one of three states:

- **Unlimited** (default): PAYG continues once packages are exhausted, with no ceiling;
- **Monthly cap**: PAYG spends at most the **amount** you set this period (entered in **¥** on the billing page; the API field is `monthly_limit_amount`);
- **PAYG forbidden**: PAYG is never used — runs stop once packages are exhausted.

Both the cap and the amount used are denominated in **money** (the billing page shows `payg_cap_amount` and `payg_used_amount`, and the input is prefixed with ¥); today's `1 credit = ¥1` rate makes the two numerically the same.

When gifted credits, purchased packages, and authorized PAYG are all insufficient, the system stops admitting new AI SRE runs. A top-level run that has already started is not interrupted, and the usage it goes on to produce is still metered. Metered is not the same as charged: **usage beyond the credits you had available and the PAYG you authorized is absorbed by Flashduty and is not billed to you** — PAYG can never be charged beyond the cap you set, and gifted or package balances are never drawn below zero.

## Billing rules

### What consumes credits

AI SRE credit consumption comes from three independently metered sources:

| Charge | How it is metered |
| --- | --- |
| Model usage | By token usage, split into fresh input, cache-hit input and output |
| Sandbox runtime | By online minute: an observed-online sandbox charges one full minute (part of a minute counts as a minute); paused time is neither charged nor backfilled |
| Web search | By the number of successful search requests |

The credit price of each kind of usage is the [price Flashduty publishes](/en/platform/pricing#ai-sre-pricing) at the time of use. Flashduty may change unit prices for future usage but does not retroactively change deductions already made. The price of model usage is set per model and does not change when the backend switches supplier or fails over to a fallback route.

Account billing pages show credits only, not model-token detail. AI SRE records the usage facts, while Flashduty's account settlement system produces settlement and bills.

Account pages round displayed amounts and credits to two decimal places. Internal metering and settlement carry considerably more precision than the display. Display precision does not change the settled result.

### What is and is not charged

- Charging starts the moment you enable AI SRE. Usage from before then is not charged, and is not charged retroactively once you enable it.
- A model call with valid token usage is charged for its model-token usage, whether the task ultimately succeeds, fails, or is stopped.
- Sandboxes are charged for **online** time. Paused time is not charged. Sandbox charging is independent of whether any task is running — an online sandbox is metered regardless — so release a sandbox once you no longer need it.
- A search request that returns an error (for example, the search service is unavailable or the request is invalid) is not charged. A search that succeeds but matches nothing is still a successful search request and is charged normally.
- AI-generated output is for reference only. You are responsible for validating it against your own business, permissions, and change-management process, and for decisions to act or not act on it.

### Allowance reminders

So that you are not cut off by a drained allowance, the system sends an automatic reminder when the **available allowance drops to 20% of the total**: the available allowance is computed across every funding source for the period — gifted credits + unexpired package balances + what remains of the PAYG cap; **a package expiring within 7 days counts as used** (it still counts toward the total, but no longer toward the available allowance). The reminder goes by **SMS and email** to the account and its enabled administrators; at most one reminder per allowance per billing period, and buying a package or raising the PAYG cap changes the allowance, so a re-armed reminder can fire again if the account is still below the threshold. An account with PAYG set to **Unlimited** never triggers this reminder (an uncapped allowance cannot run out), and neither do accounts that are not enabled or are in trial mode. This reminder concerns the AI SRE allowance for the period and is independent of the [**Low balance alert**](/en/platform/pricing#low-balance-alert) (a wallet-balance threshold you set yourself, notified by SMS + email).

### Account arrears

PAYG is settled from the account's general wallet. When the account's **available balance is negative (arrears)**, the platform disables **every pay-as-you-go (postpaid) resource** under the account (currently On-call, RUM, and AI SRE), AI SRE included — no new AI SRE run can be started while the account is in arrears; a run already under way is not interrupted and is completed and metered as usual. Gifted credits and purchased packages are **prepaid** resources: they are neither zeroed nor drawn down by arrears, and a package's balance and validity are unaffected (its expiry clock does not pause). Once you top up so the account is no longer in arrears, a sweep that runs about every 30 minutes re-enables the affected resources automatically, with no ticket required; only resources disabled because of arrears are re-enabled. See [Plans and Billing](/en/platform/pricing#arrears-and-service-suspension) for the complete arrears-suspension and recovery rules.

## Refunds

There is no self-service refund flow, and a refund is processed for a whole package only — partial refunds are not supported. To request one, contact [support@flashcat.cloud](mailto:support@flashcat.cloud) with your account, order ID, and reason for the request; Flashduty reviews eligibility manually.

A package qualifies for a refund only if it is **completely unused** (its credit balance still equals the amount originally granted — not a single credit has been drawn) **and** unexpired. A package that has had any credits drawn from it, however little, or one that has expired, cannot be refunded. An approved refund returns the full amount paid for that package's order through Flashduty's standard refund channel; the same package cannot be refunded twice. Gifted credits are not refundable.

Whether a refund is approved and its amount are subject to the review result, applicable law, and any separate agreement between you and Flashduty. Nothing in this Agreement limits any refund right that cannot be excluded by law.
