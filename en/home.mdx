## Products

### AI SRE: autonomous troubleshooting agent

Issue instructions in natural language, and the AI investigates incidents, finds root causes, calls tools, and accumulates reusable operational knowledge. It works with Flashduty incident response and IM collaboration.

- **Chat to troubleshoot**: the Agent plans, calls tools, and streams its investigation and conclusion
- **Integrated with incident response**: spin up a session from an incident or war room with full context
- **Knowledge that compounds**: DUTY.md-rooted knowledge holds long-lived operational context
- **Extensible tooling**: Skills, MCP, A2A Agents, and self-hosted Runners

AI SRE is available to accounts on an On-call Pro or higher subscription and is billed on actual usage. See the [billing terms](/en/compliance/ai-sre-credit-package-purchase-agreement).

[Introduction](/en/ai-sre) · [Product overview](/en/ai-sre/overview) · [Quickstart](/en/ai-sre/quickstart)

### On-call: alert response

Reduce noise, schedule, assign, escalate, and notify so teams can respond to production incidents quickly.

- **Noise reduction**: alert grouping, inhibition, and deduplication cut repeated notifications
- **Flexible assignment**: multi-level escalation, dynamic routing, and rotation scheduling
- **Multi-channel notifications**: Feishu/Lark, DingTalk, WeCom, Slack, Microsoft Teams, phone calls, SMS, and email
- **Rich integrations**: 290+ alert integrations covering mainstream monitoring tools and cloud platforms

[Introduction](/en/on-call) · [Getting started](/en/on-call/quickstart/quickstart) · [Compare with PagerDuty / Opsgenie](/en/on-call/comparison/vs-pagerduty)

### RUM: real user monitoring

Understand how real users experience your application and quickly find and fix frontend issues.

- **Performance monitoring**: full-chain tracking of page loads, resource loads, and API calls
- **Error tracking**: automatic collection and grouping of JS errors and network errors
- **Session replay**: reproduce user paths to replicate issues quickly
- **Custom metrics**: report business-specific metrics

[Introduction](/en/rum) · [Getting started](/en/rum/quickstart/quickstart) · [Performance monitoring](/en/rum/performance/overview)

### Monitors: alert engine

Connect data sources across network zones and manage alert rules in one place. An alert engine next to your data evaluates the rules locally, and the resulting events flow into On-call.

- **Multiple data source types**: Prometheus, VictoriaLogs, Loki, Elasticsearch, SLS, and Tencent Cloud CLS for metrics and logs, plus MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle, ClickHouse, Redis, Kafka, and MongoDB for databases and middleware
- **Local evaluation next to your data**: `monitedge` runs in your private network, syncs rules from SaaS, and queries data sources locally, so data stays in your network. Engine instances in a cluster shard the rules automatically
- **Three evaluation modes**: threshold, no-data, and any-data, with consecutive or cumulative hit counting and per-level recovery semantics
- **Unified management views**: manage rules in a folder tree, with overview, active alerts, Query Workbench, Entity Tree and dashboards, plus rule import/export and change audit
