## Introduction

---

In the era of digital services, a **status page** is a critical bridge for building service transparency and trust between businesses and their users. When service disruptions or planned maintenance occur, a clear, timely, and subscribable status page significantly reduces customer anxiety, cuts down on support tickets, and demonstrates professional incident communication.

<Note>
**Core Question**: Is your status page just a standalone bulletin board, or is it an integral part of your incident response system?
</Note>

### Are You Facing These Challenges?

When your organization encounters the following situations, it's time to consider adopting or upgrading your status page tool:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Service Transparency" icon="eye">
    Customers cannot get timely service status updates during outages, leading to floods of duplicate support tickets and complaints
  </Card>
  <Card title="Disconnected Communication" icon="link-slash">
    External status pages and internal incident response operate in silos, with unsynchronized information and inefficient communication
  </Card>
  <Card title="SLA Compliance Tracking" icon="scale-balanced">
    Lacking complete incident history and availability statistics, making it difficult to track SLA achievement and historical incident impact
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscriptions & Notifications" icon="bell">
    Unable to manage subscribers granularly or push status changes through commonly used enterprise IM channels
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Procurement Guide Overview

---

This guide provides in-depth analysis of what enterprises should focus on when choosing a status page tool, from three core dimensions: **Product Features**, **Pricing Strategy**, and **Migration Compatibility**.

We'll also provide a detailed comparison between **Flashduty Status Page** and **Atlassian Statuspage** to help you make an informed choice.

<Tip>
**Positioning Difference**: Flashduty Status Page is designed as part of the incident response system, while Atlassian Statuspage is oriented as a standalone status communication page. Flashduty's core advantage lies not just in having more features, but in tightly integrating status pages, subscriptions, incidents, maintenance, historical statistics, internal notifications, and migration compatibility into a cohesive system.
</Tip>

## Product Feature Comparison

---

### Status Page Types & Communication

A status page serves not only as an information window for external users—it's equally important for internal team status synchronization.

| Core Capability | Key Evaluation Questions | Flashduty vs Atlassian Statuspage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public Status Page** | Does it support public-facing status pages for customers and partners? | **Both support** |
| **Internal Status Page** | Does it support private status pages for internal employees? | Flashduty has built-in internal status pages with IM channel notifications; Atlassian's Private and Audience-specific pages are billed under separate models |
| **Unified Management** | Are public and internal pages managed within the same product? | ✅ **Flashduty advantage**: unified management within one product. Atlassian Audience-specific is a separate product line |

## Pricing Comparison

---

Pricing is a critical factor in enterprise decisions—you need to choose the most cost-effective option that meets your requirements.

| Dimension | Flashduty Status Page | Atlassian Statuspage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pricing Page** | [flashcat.cloud/flashduty/price](https://flashcat.cloud/flashduty/price/) | [atlassian.com/statuspage/pricing](https://www.atlassian.com/software/statuspage/pricing) |
| **Billing Model** | Included in the On-call module, not sold separately | Standalone product, tiered by subscribers, components, team members, etc. |
| **Traffic Limits** | ✅ **Unlimited traffic for all status page types** | Limited by plan |
| **Public Page** | ✅ Included in On-call module | Tiered pricing by subscribers, components, team members, and metrics |
| **Private Page** | ✅ Included in On-call module | Tiered by authenticated subscribers |
| **Component Subscription** | ✅ Available in all editions | Business and above only |

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Atlassian Model">
    Atlassian Statuspage is billed as a standalone product, with pricing based on multiple dimensions:
    - **Public page**: tiered by subscribers, components, team members, and metrics
    - **Private page**: tiered by authenticated subscribers
    - **Audience-specific page**: separate product line, billed independently from the start
    - Full feature access requires **Business or Enterprise** plans
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Flashduty Model">
    Flashduty Status Page is provided as part of the On-call module:
    - Not sold separately—**included in the On-call subscription**
    - **Unlimited traffic** for all status page types
    - Public and internal status pages are **managed together** with no additional cost
    - Advanced features like component-level subscriptions are **not gated by plan tiers**
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
**Key Difference**: Flashduty Status Page is a built-in capability of the On-call module—no separate purchase needed. Atlassian Statuspage is a standalone product where full feature access requires higher-tier plans.
</Tip>

## Summary and Recommendations

### Core Advantages Comparison

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flashduty Highlights" icon="star">

  <Card title="Atlassian Statuspage Strengths" icon="globe">

### Selection Recommendations

We recommend making your decision based on the following factors:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Product Positioning">
    Do you need a standalone status communication page, or a status page integrated with your incident response system?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Internal & External Communication">
    Do you need status pages that serve both external customers and internal teams?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notification Channels">
    Does your team rely on Feishu/Lark, Dingtalk, WeCom, or other regional IM tools?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Total Cost">
    Should the status page be purchased separately, or is it more cost-effective as part of your On-call capabilities?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Migration Cost">
    Are you migrating from Atlassian Statuspage? How comprehensive are the available migration tools?
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Final Recommendation**: We suggest **fully evaluating both products** before making the most suitable choice based on your team's actual needs, existing toolchain, and budget constraints. If you're already using or planning to use Flashduty On-call, the status page is available as a built-in capability at no additional cost.
</Warning>
