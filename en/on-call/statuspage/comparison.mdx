This page compares Flashduty Status Page with Atlassian Statuspage across incidents, subscriptions, display, notifications, and pricing, and covers migration.

- **Flashduty Status Page**: part of the incident response system, with status pages, subscriptions, incidents, maintenance, historical statistics, and internal notifications in one product. **Included in the On-call module at no extra cost**.
- **Atlassian Statuspage**: a standalone status communication page with a full feature set, but **billed as a separate product**, with complete capabilities requiring higher-tier plans.

A self-built status page shares infrastructure with your business services and may go down with them during an outage; this page compares third-party products only.

## Pricing comparison

Atlassian Statuspage is billed as a standalone product, with pricing increasing across tiers based on subscribers, components, team members, and more. Flashduty Status Page is **included in the On-call module at no extra cost**.

| Dimension | Flashduty Status Page | Atlassian Statuspage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pricing Page** | [flashcat.cloud/flashduty/price](https://flashcat.cloud/flashduty/price/) | [atlassian.com/statuspage/pricing](https://www.atlassian.com/software/statuspage/pricing) |
| **Billing Model** | **Included in the On-call module**, not sold separately | Standalone product, tiered across multiple dimensions |
| **Traffic Limits** | ✅ **Free** | Limited by plan |
| **Public Page** | ✅ Free | Tiered by subscribers, components, team members, and metrics |

| **Component Subscription** | ✅ All editions | Business and above only |
| **Component Grouping** | ✅ All editions | Business and above only |

## Flashduty status page reliability

The core-function availability and alert delivery time commitments in the [Service Level Agreement (SLA)](/en/compliance/service-sla) apply to paid Flashduty services; they do not apply to the free plan or to self-hosted deployments.

## Migrating from Atlassian Statuspage

Flashduty CLI migrates components, sections, incident history, and subscribers (email + best-effort Microsoft Teams Workflows webhooks) from Atlassian Statuspage in two independent steps (Pro plan required): verify imported structure and history first, then import subscribers without sending duplicate notifications. It also keeps the `history.rss` and `history.atom` link formats, so existing RSS/Atom subscribers change nothing.

For steps, command flags, and a full walkthrough, see the migration section in [Get started with Flashduty Status Page](/en/on-call/statuspage/get-started).
