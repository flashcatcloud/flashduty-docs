Flashduty Status Pages allow users to actively subscribe to service status updates. When a new event is published or an event status changes, you can choose to push notifications to subscribers.

---

## Subscription methods

Different status page types support different subscription methods:

| Status page type | Subscription method | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | Email | Visitors subscribe with an email address; email confirmation is required |
| **Public** | Slack | Visitors authorize the Flashduty Status Page Slack app to push updates into a Slack channel; no email confirmation required |
| **Public** | Microsoft Teams | Visitors subscribe a Teams channel using a Microsoft Teams Workflows webhook URL; no email confirmation required |
| **Public** | RSS/Atom | Subscribe to event updates through any RSS reader or automation tool; no confirmation required |
| **Internal** | IM integration | Internal users receive push notifications through linked IM platforms |

The subscription dialog displays component and section names in the visitor's active page language and records their locale preference. Notifications render the status page title and affected component names in that locale, or clamp to the page's sole enabled language if the subscriber's locale is disabled. See [Multilingual status pages](/en/on-call/statuspage/multilingual).

## User self-service subscription

### Email subscription (public status pages)

When a visitor enters an email address on a public status page, the system sends a confirmation email. The confirmation email is valid for **5 minutes**; after expiration, the visitor must resubmit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter email address">
    The visitor enters their email address in the subscription area at the bottom of the status page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose subscription scope">
    Select whether to subscribe to all updates, or only updates for specific components or events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm subscription">
    The visitor clicks the confirmation link in the received email to complete the subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
When a pending subscription already exists for the same email address, submitting the form again neither changes the scope awaiting confirmation nor sends another confirmation email — the scope chosen on the first submission is what takes effect. To change it, resubmit after the confirmation email expires (5 minutes). A confirmed subscriber who changes scope must resubmit the form and click the link in the new confirmation email: their existing subscription stays as it is until they confirm, and the new scope replaces it once confirmed. Alternatively, they can use the manage token in any notification email to update it directly.
</Note>

### Slack subscription (public status pages)

When Slack subscriptions are enabled on a public status page, visitors can route incident and maintenance updates directly into a Slack channel:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose subscription scope">
    Open the subscription dialog on the status page, switch to the **Slack** tab, and choose all updates or specific components.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize in Slack">
    Click **Add to Slack**, pick the target workspace and channel on the Slack authorization page, and approve the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive updates">
    The subscription takes effect immediately. Every Slack notification card includes links to manage subscription scope or unsubscribe.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Microsoft Teams subscription (public status pages)

When Microsoft Teams subscriptions are enabled on a public status page, visitors can receive Adaptive Card updates in a Teams channel via **Microsoft Teams Workflows**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Workflows webhook URL in Teams">
    In Microsoft Teams, open the target channel's **More options (`...`) → Workflows → "Send webhook alerts to a channel"** (or **"Post to a channel when a webhook request is received"**) and copy the generated webhook URL (`*.logic.azure.com` or `*.environment.api.powerplatform.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit on the status page">
    Open the subscription dialog on the status page, switch to the **Teams** tab, paste the Workflows webhook URL, choose all updates or specific components, and submit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Legacy Office 365 Connector webhook URLs (`*.webhook.office.com`) have been retired by Microsoft and are rejected. Use a Teams Workflows webhook URL instead.
</Note>

### IM subscription (internal status pages)

Internal organization users can receive event push notifications on their linked IM integrations (Feishu/Lark, Dingtalk, WeCom, Slack) after accessing the internal status page. IM subscriptions do not require email confirmation.

### RSS/Atom subscription (public status pages)

Every public status page has an event feed in RSS and Atom formats. Visitors can subscribe with any RSS reader (Feedly, Inoreader, and so on) or automation tool. Feeds are anonymous and need no account or email confirmation.

Each public status page serves the following four URLs. They return identical content; the `history.*` pair exists as an alias so that Atlassian Statuspage subscribers can migrate without changing their feed URLs:

| URL pattern | Format |
| --- | --- |
| `{page-url}/feed.rss` | RSS 2.0 |
| `{page-url}/feed.atom` | Atom 1.0 |
| `{page-url}/history.rss` | RSS 2.0 (alias) |
| `{page-url}/history.atom` | Atom 1.0 (alias) |

`{page-url}` is the public URL of the status page — either the custom domain (for example `https://status.example.com`) or the default Flashduty-assigned URL.

Feed contents follow these rules:

- Includes events published within the last **90 days** (both incidents and maintenance), up to **50 items**, ordered by start time descending
- Only visible component impacts appear in the feed: the feed leaves out hidden components and sections
- Each item's `<updated>` timestamp reflects the most recent timeline update, so readers can detect new activity on refresh
- Responses support `ETag` / `If-None-Match` and return `304 Not Modified` when nothing has changed
- Accepts an optional `?lang=zh` or `?lang=en` query parameter (for example, `{page-url}/feed.rss?lang=zh` or `{page-url}/feed.atom?lang=en`) to return localized status page titles and affected component names (event titles and update messages stay as authored). Single-language pages ignore disabled-language parameters and always return the enabled language; bilingual pages default to English when `?lang=` is omitted. See [Multilingual status pages](/en/on-call/statuspage/multilingual)

<Note>
Feeds are available for **public** status pages only. Requesting a feed URL for an internal or non-existent page returns `404 Not Found`. For internal pages, use IM integrations instead.
</Note>

<Tip>
If you are migrating from Atlassian Statuspage, you can point the existing `history.rss` / `history.atom` subscription URLs at your Flashduty status page. Subscribers change nothing.
</Tip>

## Subscription scope

Subscribers can flexibly choose their subscription scope:

| Scope | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **All updates** | Receive notifications for all events on the status page |
| **By component** | Receive notifications only for events related to specific components |
| **By event** | Receive only subsequent update notifications for specific events |

When "All updates" is selected, Flashduty ignores component-level and event-level subscription settings — the subscriber receives all notifications.

## Administrator subscription management

### Subscription settings

On the **Subscriptions** page of the status page settings, administrators control subscription availability per channel:

- **Public status pages**: independent switches for **Email notifications**, **Slack notifications**, and **Microsoft Teams notifications**. Turning off a channel hides that tab from visitors and stops sending notifications through it, even if "Notify subscribers" is checked when publishing an event.
- **Internal status pages**: a page-level switch for **IM notifications**.

Usage rules for subscription notifications:

- **Email (public status pages)**: event notification emails count toward the Flashduty account's email quota. For Free plan accounts, Flashduty stops sending event notification emails once the quota is exceeded. For paid plan accounts, emails beyond the quota are billed separately, and notifications stop only when the account is in arrears.
- **Slack & Microsoft Teams (public status pages)**: notifications are delivered directly to subscribed channels and do not consume your Flashduty email quota.
- **IM (internal status pages)**: event notifications count toward the API call quota of the corresponding IM platform; when the IM platform's API quota is exceeded, Flashduty stops sending notifications. API call limits usually depend on the IM platform pricing plan your organization uses.

### View subscribers

In the Subscriber List section of the **Subscriptions** page in the status page settings, administrators can view the current subscriber list and filter by **component** and **subscription method** (Email, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or IM). The list supports pagination and displays each subscriber's subscription method, recipient label (such as email address, `#channel (Workspace)` for Slack, or `Teams Webhook (<id>)` for Teams), subscribed components, and language preference.

### Bulk import

<Tip>
**Plan requirement**: bulk importing subscribers requires the Flashduty **Pro** plan.
</Tip>

Administrators can bulk import subscribers, useful for migration scenarios or pre-configuring subscriptions for specific user groups. The console has no import entry; import through the API (see the [bulk import subscribers API](/en/api-reference/on-call/status-pages/status-page-subscriber-import)). The following fields are required for import:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Recipient** | Email address or user identifier |
| **Subscription scope** | All updates, or specific component IDs / event IDs |
| **Locale** | Language preference for notifications |

<Note>
Subscriptions imported by administrators do not require email confirmation and take effect immediately. Importing a subscription for the same recipient replaces that recipient's previous subscription settings.
</Note>

### Export subscribers

<Tip>
**Plan requirement**: exporting subscribers requires the Flashduty **Standard** plan or above.
</Tip>

Administrators can click **Export CSV** in the **Subscribers** list to export the subscriber list, including each subscriber's recipient address, subscription method, and subscribed components.

## Subscriber self-management

Each subscriber has a unique **manage token** that allows them to self-manage their subscription:

- **View current subscription**: See which components and events they are subscribed to
- **Modify subscription scope**: Change subscribed components or events
- **Unsubscribe**: Completely opt out of the subscription

Every notification email includes the manage token at the bottom. Subscribers can click the "Manage subscription" link to access the self-management page.

### One-click unsubscribe from the mail client

Event change-notification emails support one-click unsubscribe. Mainstream mail clients (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook, Yahoo) show an "Unsubscribe" button at the top of the message, and clicking it removes the subscription immediately.

<Warning>
One-click unsubscribe is **immediate and irreversible**: the request takes effect on arrival, no management page is involved, and no confirmation step is sent. To re-subscribe, the user must resubmit their email address on the status page and complete the confirmation flow again.
</Warning>

## Subscriptions and component lifecycle

When you delete a component, the system automatically removes all subscription records related to that component. Subscribers who were subscribed to that component will no longer receive related notifications.

Similarly, when you delete an event, the system automatically removes all subscription records related to that event.
