## Overview

Action items track specific work during incident handling, such as "scale up the database connection pool" or "roll back the latest release". Manage them in the **Actions** card on the right of the incident overview page. Each item has a title, a description, and one or more assignees.

You can convert an action item into a **follow-up**, which then enters the post-mortem process. Follow-ups use the same fields and statuses as action items. Action items cover handling; follow-ups cover improvement work after the incident ends. You can create a follow-up directly on a closed incident without a post-mortem report. You can also maintain it in the report's **To-dos** block; see [Converting to follow-ups and linking to post-mortems](#converting-to-follow-ups-and-linking-to-post-mortems).

## Overview panel

The right side of the incident overview page shows two cards, **Actions** and **Follow-ups**, whose editable phases are complementary:

| Card | Editable phase |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Actions** | While the incident is open |
| **Follow-ups** | After the incident is closed |

The card that is not editable is read-only and shows existing items only. The editable card comes first.

Both cards use the same inline operations (edit, delete, complete, mark as won't do). The sections below use action items as the example; the same operations apply to follow-ups.

## Creating and editing action items

### Create

In the **Actions** card on the incident overview page, click **Add an action** (or **Add** if the card has items). Then fill in these fields:

| Field | Required | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Title** | Yes | A brief description of the work to be done, up to **512** characters |
| **Description** | No | Additional details about what needs to be done, up to **65535** characters |
| **Assignees** | No | Search and multi-select from account members; no limit on the number. You can also pick the **AI SRE** option pinned at the top of the list (sub-label **Assign to AI**) to hand the item to the account's AI SRE |

Once created, the action item appears in the card and the incident timeline records an "action created" event. Members assigned as assignees receive a personal notification prompting them to follow through (sent through **email and in-app push** by default; you can adjust notification channels in [Personal Settings](/en/on-call/configuration/personal-settings)).

### Assigning to AI SRE

The assignee picker pins **AI SRE** above the member list, separated from it by a divider. Search never filters it out, so it stays in the results whatever you type. Selecting it hands the item to the account's AI SRE, and you can keep member assignees alongside it. Action items and follow-ups share the same create/edit dialog, so the field behaves the same for both.

- The card shows the assignment as an **AI SRE** avatar next to the member avatars (up to 2 avatars, with the rest collapsed into `+N`)
- Hovering the AI SRE avatar opens a hover card that names the member whose identity and permissions the AI SRE runs with, and offers **Continue conversation**, which opens the session behind this run in the AI SRE panel
- When the run came from an IM session, the hover card only says **IM sessions can't be viewed on the web yet** and offers no entry point
- Opening the session requires AI SRE to be enabled for the account and AI SRE chat permission for you; personal sessions (visible only to their creator) and expired sessions can't be continued
- On the incident timeline, this work item's record lists the assignees with a joint signature (for example, "John and AI SRE")

To list everything handed to AI SRE, use `flashduty incident work-item-list --assignee-type ai_sre` in the [CLI](/en/developer/cli).

### Edit and delete

- Members with incident update permission can click the item title, or use **Edit** in the **More** menu, to change the title, description, or assignees
- Newly added assignees also receive a personal notification; removing an assignee does not trigger a notification
- **Delete** in the **More** menu requires confirmation; deletion cannot be undone

## Status transitions

Manage each action item's status with its checkbox and the **More** menu:

| Action | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| Check the checkbox | Marks the item as **Completed**; the entry is displayed in a muted style |
| Uncheck the checkbox | **Cancel completion**, returning the item to the open state |
| **More** → **Mark as won't do** | Marks the item as **Won't do**; the title is displayed with a strikethrough |
| Click the X icon on a won't-do item | Reopens the action item |

The **More** menu also provides **Mark as completed** and **Cancel completion** shortcuts. Status changes are likewise recorded on the incident timeline.

## Converting to follow-ups and linking to post-mortems

Follow-ups belong to an incident that has ended. The system converts unfinished action items automatically when the incident closes, and you can keep adding follow-ups afterwards:

- **Automatic conversion on close**: When an incident closes, Flashduty converts all its unfinished action items into follow-ups at once. It writes one timeline record: **converted N unfinished actions to follow-ups: title, title…**. Completed and won't-do action items stay as read-only action items
- **Automatic post-mortem linking**: If the incident has a post-mortem report, Flashduty links new or converted follow-ups to it. Otherwise, follow-ups stay unlinked until someone creates a report, and then Flashduty links them all
- **Working in the post-mortem report**: You can also edit, complete, or delete linked follow-ups in the report's **To-dos** block. The incident timeline records these operations

For writing and publishing post-mortems, see [Create and Edit Post-Mortem Reports](/en/on-call/post-mortem/create-edit-post-mortem).

## Permission model

Access to action items and follow-ups follows these rules:

| Role / scenario | Capability |
| :--- | :--- |
| Members with incident update permission | While the incident is open: create, edit, and delete action items and perform any status transition; after it is closed: create, edit, and delete follow-ups and perform any status transition |
| Action item / follow-up assignees | Even without incident update permission, assignees can mark their own items as **Completed** (they cannot edit content or delete) |
| Follow-ups linked to a post-mortem report | Editing and deleting require edit permission on the corresponding post-mortem report; **completing** only requires being an assignee, whether or not the follow-up is linked |
| Follow-ups not linked to a post-mortem report | Verified against incident update permission; this covers follow-ups created directly on a closed incident whose post-mortem report does not exist yet |

Access to action items also follows the incident's **collaboration space**. Only account owners, account admins, and members with access to that space can work on them. Manual incidents without a collaboration space are visible to all account members. This matches the access rules of the incident itself.

**Assignees** in the table above means account members. A work item assigned to **AI SRE** is executed by the AI: AI SRE is not an account member, so the member-facing completion permission and personal notification above don't apply to it (when the same item also has member assignees, those members are notified as usual).

## Closed incidents

Once an incident is closed, the Actions card becomes read-only while the Follow-ups card unlocks for editing:

- **Actions**: You can't create, edit, or delete action items or change their status, and the API rejects creating them too. This card usually holds only completed and won't-do items
- **Follow-ups**: You can add, edit, complete, or delete them directly
- To keep an item as an action item, mark it **Completed** or **Won't do** before closing; otherwise, closing converts it into a follow-up
