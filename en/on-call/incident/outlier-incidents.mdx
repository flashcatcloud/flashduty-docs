<Tip>**Plan requirement**: This feature requires an On-call Pro or higher subscription. [Learn more](https://flashcat.cloud/flashduty/price/)</Tip>

An outlier incident is one that has not occurred in the past period. There is no handling experience to draw on, its impact may be unknown, and the response may need to be decided on the spot. Flashduty marks these incidents with an **Outlier** badge so responders can pay closer attention, or focus on them and write SOPs for them.

### Recognition method

The system compares the semantic similarity of the incident title, description, affected service, and alert resources. Incidents with similarity above 90% are treated as similar; see [Past Incidents · Recognition method](/en/on-call/incident/past-incidents#recognition-method).

<Note>
When the system detects no similar incidents in the past **30** days, it marks the incident as an outlier.
</Note>

## Enable and disable

You configure outlier incident detection per channel. It is on by default for Pro and higher plans:

<Steps>
<Step title="Enter Channel Settings">
Go to **Channel** → Select target channel → **Configuration** → **Settings** → **Advanced Configuration**
</Step>
<Step title="Configure Detection Toggle">
Find **Outlier Incident Detection** option, enable or disable the feature
</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
When enabled, incident lists and notification content will include an "Outlier" badge for quick identification. See [Create and Manage Channels](/en/on-call/channel/create-edit) for details.
</Tip>
