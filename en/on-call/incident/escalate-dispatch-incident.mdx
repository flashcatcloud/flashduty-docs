## Reassign

If an incident assigned to you isn't yours to handle, reassign it to other people or schedules.

Reassigning means re-notifying. If reassigning a **Processing** incident, it may reset progress to **Triggered**. Unless the new responders include previous responders who already acknowledged.

<Note>
Current reassignment targets can only be schedules or individuals, called **Direct Assignment**. The difference from **Policy Assignment** is that directly assigned incidents can only be notified per personal preferences and cannot be escalated.
</Note>

## Escalate

| Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Auto Escalate** | When an incident is auto-assigned to you by a (multi-level) escalation rule, you don't resolve it within the specified time window, you don't snooze, and it doesn't auto-recover, the incident auto-escalates to the next level at the specified time |
| **Manual Escalate** | When an incident is auto-assigned to you by a (multi-level) escalation rule, but you confirm you cannot handle it after receiving the alert and need next level (for example, tier-2 support) to intervene early, you can choose to directly escalate to another level |

<Tip>
Escalation is essentially **Reassignment**. The main difference is that **Escalation** assigns and notifies per the personnel and notification channels already defined in the escalation rule.
</Tip>

## Add responders

To bring in people who aren't responders yet, add them as responders.

Similar to **Reassign**, adding responders only allows selecting schedules or individuals as targets. The system notifies these personnel per their personal preferences.

<Tip>
These operations are also available in the [Flashduty App](/en/on-call/quickstart/mobile-app): on the incident detail page, **Reassign** and **Escalate** are under **More actions (⋮)**, and **Add responders** is in the **Responders** panel. For a **closed** incident, the App hides **Add responders** and **Reassign**. The **Responders** panel becomes a read-only list that still shows who acknowledged the incident and when.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why doesn't adding responders notify per policy?">
Adding responders and reassigning default to personal notification preferences. You can update this in personal settings. In the future, we'll support modifying notification methods when assigning.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is there no escalate button in my incident details?">
Only when an incident is assigned by escalation rules can you switch between different levels. When you choose direct assignment (for example, specified an individual with reassign), the incident has no association with any escalation rule and cannot escalate through policy.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
