## Why post-mortems matter

A post-mortem reviews an incident after it is over and answers three questions: **What happened, why it happened, and how to prevent it from happening again.**

In Flashduty, post-mortems are part of incident management. Create a report from the incident detail page in one click, let AI write the first draft, refine it with your team, and archive the result.

## What does a post-mortem report look like

A typical post-mortem report contains:

- **Linked incident**: Which incident this report is written for
- **Basic info**: The linked incident's severity, progress, and responders
- **Report body**: A detailed analysis organized by template sections — typically including summary, timeline, impact, contributors, root cause, mitigators, and learnings and risks
- **Follow-ups**: Action items that need to be carried out after the review
- **Author list**: Everyone who contributed to writing the report

### Report status

A post-mortem report has two statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Drafting** | The report is still being written and can be freely edited |
| **Published** | The report is finalized and available for the team to review |

<Tip>
You can switch between drafting and published at any time. If you need to add something after publishing, click **Re-edit**, make your changes, and republish.
</Tip>

### Ownership

When a post-mortem report is created, it automatically inherits the channel and team from the linked incident, making it easy to manage and browse reports by team.
