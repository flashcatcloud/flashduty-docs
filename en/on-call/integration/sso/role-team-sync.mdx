On every sign-in, SAML2.0, OIDC, and CAS sync a member's roles and teams from the role and team names the identity provider returns. For how to set the sync switches, role and team fields, and defaults, the overwrite rules, and when defaults apply, see [Role and team mapping](/en/platform/configure-sso#role-and-team-mapping). LDAP maps by Group DN instead; see [LDAP role and team synchronization](/en/platform/configure-sso#ldap-role-and-team-synchronization).

<Warning>
Leave default roles and default teams empty in most cases. Don't configure administrator roles or sensitive teams just to make a sign-in test work.
</Warning>

## Where each protocol reads names

The names you enter in **Role field** and **Team field** refer to these locations:

| Protocol | Source of role and team names |
| --- | --- |
| SAML2.0 | An attribute in the SAML assertion; the attribute name is the name you enter, such as `roles` |
| OIDC | A claim in the ID Token; the claim name is the name you enter. When sync is on and the ID Token doesn't carry the claim, Flashduty also calls the **UserInfo** endpoint and matches against the merged attributes and claims |
| CAS | An attribute in the CAS `/serviceValidate` response; the attribute name is the name you enter |

## Name matching details

- A value can be a single string or an array of strings. Non-string array elements are ignored.
- Names are trimmed and matched exactly, case-sensitive: `Ops` and `ops` are different names.
- The preset roles `Admin`, `Responder`, and `Viewer` always match. A custom role must be enabled; a disabled role doesn't match even with the same name.
- Only teams that haven't been deleted match.
- When several roles or teams share a name, the one with the smallest ID is used.
- Names that match nothing are skipped and don't affect other names.
- A role or team selected as a default can't be deleted until you clear it in the SSO configuration. See [When defaults apply](/en/platform/configure-sso#when-defaults-apply).
