## Get the app from Microsoft Teams

<Steps>
<Step title="Find Flashduty">
In Microsoft Teams, select **Apps**, search for **Flashduty**, and open its details page.
</Step>

<Step title="Add the App">
Select **Add**. Choose the destination that matches your use case: a team/channel, a group chat, or your personal chat.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
In the commands below, replace `{ID}` with the Flashduty Microsoft Teams integration ID. Replace `{ChatName}` with the group chat name.
</Note>

## Notification targets

The top of the **Configuration** tab on the integration detail page is the **Notification targets** card: Flashduty sends incident notifications only to the teams and group chats listed here. The card has two tabs, **Teams** and **Group chat**, and the number after each tab label is how many targets are linked:

- **Teams** lists each linked team with the channels the Flashduty app was read from in that team (shown as `Channel: General`). When no channel can be read, the row warns that the Flashduty app may have been removed from the team; select **Retry** to read that team again.
- Both tabs embed a three-step guide for adding a target: install the app in Teams and pick the destination (a team must use the General channel), send the link command in the target conversation, then select **Link Now** in the bot reply.
- **Unlink** on a row opens a confirmation dialog that states the team or group chat stops receiving incident notifications once unlinked; select **Confirm** in the dialog to actually unlink. The dialog also folds in "How to remove Flashduty Bot after unlinking?".

Below that card is the **Advanced Features** card, which configures this integration's AI SRE capabilities.

## Link team

<Steps>
<Step title="Find App">
In Teams, open **Apps**, find **Flashduty**, and select **Add to a team**.
</Step>

<Step title="Add to Team">
Add the app to the target Team.

<Warning>
This step must select the target Team's General Channel, otherwise incidents cannot be sent to the Team.
</Warning>

![Microsoft Teams: Add to Team screenshot](https://docs-cdn.flashcat.cloud/images/png/01fa86b63d01d2735aa6c4a53efb3c69.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Send Link Command">
In the Team's General channel, type @Flashduty and send `linkTeam {ID}`, then select **Link Now** in the bot reply. You must type @Flashduty yourself and pick the bot from the popup list; a pasted @ is not a mention.

Selecting **Link Now** brings you back to the integration detail page and completes the link automatically, so the team appears under **Notification targets** > **Teams**.

![Microsoft Teams: Send Link Command screenshot](https://docs-cdn.flashcat.cloud/images/png/3192b5481b0595fcb58e5cc43abad125.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Unlink the Team">
To remove this Team's link, go to **Integration Center → Instant Messaging → Microsoft Teams → Configuration → Notification targets → Teams**, select **Unlink** on the team, and then select **Confirm** in the dialog. You can also send `@Flashduty unlinkTeam {ID}` in the same channel and follow the prompts on the response card.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Link chat

<Steps>
<Step title="Find App">
In Teams, open **Apps**, find **Flashduty**, and select **Add to a chat**.
</Step>

<Step title="Add to Chat">
Add the app to the target Chat.

![Microsoft Teams: Add to Chat screenshot](https://docs-cdn.flashcat.cloud/images/png/6e56d7de341737fe495e5ff18eb1af34.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Send Link Command">
First fill in **Chat name** in the input below the step (it is only used to display the chat in Flashduty, and the command on the page can't be copied until it's filled in). Then, in the group chat, type @Flashduty and send `linkChat {ID} {ChatName}`, and select **Link Now** in the bot reply. You must type @Flashduty yourself and pick the bot from the popup list; a pasted @ is not a mention.

Selecting **Link Now** brings you back to the integration detail page and completes the link automatically, so the group chat appears under **Notification targets** > **Group chat**.

![Microsoft Teams: Send Link Command screenshot](https://docs-cdn.flashcat.cloud/images/png/d0beee141db63714ccecb095affee79b.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Unlink the Chat">
To remove this chat's link, go to **Integration Center → Instant Messaging → Microsoft Teams → Configuration → Notification targets → Group chat**, select **Unlink** on the group chat, and then select **Confirm** in the dialog. You can also send `@Flashduty unlinkChat {ID}` in the same group chat and follow the prompts on the response card.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Notification card actions

When incident notifications are pushed to Microsoft Teams, the notification cards may include the following interactive actions. The available buttons depend on the Flashduty card you receive and the backend configuration:

- **Acknowledge**: Mark that you have started handling the incident
- **Resolve**: Mark the incident as resolved and close it
- **Snooze**: Temporarily suspend the incident and receive a reminder after a specified time
- **Custom Actions**: Trigger your pre-configured custom actions (such as restarting services, rolling back changes, and so on)

<Note>
War room functionality is not currently supported for Microsoft Teams. If you need to use the war room feature, consider using Slack, Feishu/Lark, Dingtalk, or WeCom integration instead.
</Note>

## Link user

<Steps>
<Step title="Find App">
In Teams, open **Apps**, find **Flashduty**, and select **Open**.
</Step>

<Step title="Open App">
Click **Open App**.

![Microsoft Teams: Open App screenshot](https://docs-cdn.flashcat.cloud/images/png/2e6862103d718a913d2b3c449cbf2366.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Send Link Command">
Send `linkUser {ID}` in your personal chat with Flashduty, then select **Link Now** in the bot reply.

Selecting **Link Now** brings you back to the integration detail page, landing on the **Linked users** tab where the link completes.

![Microsoft Teams: Send Link Command screenshot](https://docs-cdn.flashcat.cloud/images/png/671ae7883bbba839419e539762db99de.png)
</Step>

<Step title="Unlink Your Account">
To remove your Teams account link, send `unlinkUser {ID}` in the same personal chat. After you select **Unlink in FlashDuty** in the response card, Flashduty removes the link automatically and shows a success message. If your account is linked, you can also unlink it in the console. Go to **Integration Center → Instant Messaging → Microsoft Teams → Linked Users**, select **Unlink me**, and then select **Confirm**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Data and permissions

The Flashduty Teams app processes only the data needed to send alert notifications, complete linking configuration, and handle incident card actions. It doesn't read Teams chats unrelated to Flashduty, and it doesn't request Microsoft Graph permissions for reading organization-wide chat content. For the data it handles, see the [Flashduty Microsoft Teams app privacy policy](/en/compliance/microsoft-teams-app-privacy-policy) and [Flashduty Microsoft Teams app terms of use](/en/compliance/microsoft-teams-app-terms-of-use).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Team or individual not receiving messages?">
Go to Integration Center → Instant Messaging → **Microsoft Teams**, check if teams and users have been successfully linked.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to view linked teams and users?">
Go to Integration Center → Instant Messaging → **Microsoft Teams** → the **Configuration** tab, and view linked teams and group chats under **Notification targets** > **Teams** and **Notification targets** > **Group chat**. Users are listed on the **Linked users** tab.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to unlink teams and users?">
Unlink teams and group chats on the **Configuration** tab under **Notification targets**: open the **Teams** or **Group chat** tab, select **Unlink** on the entry, and then select **Confirm** in the dialog. You can also use the matching command in its original Teams context: `unlinkTeam {ID}` in a Team channel, `unlinkChat {ID}` in a group chat, or `unlinkUser {ID}` in your personal chat, and follow the prompts on the response card. If your account is already linked, you can also select **Unlink me** under **Linked users**, then select **Confirm** in the dialog.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
