## Product introduction

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="what-is-on-call" title="What is Flashduty On-call?">
    Flashduty On-call is a one-stop alert response platform. It gives IT teams on-call scheduling, noise reduction, escalation, and automation to speed up incident response and reduce losses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion id="is-it-for-me" title="Is Flashduty On-call right for me?">
    Flashduty On-call is a good fit if your team:

    - Needs one place to collect alerts from every source in the company for unified handling, tracking, and analysis
    - Needs to grade alerts by severity, notify key people through different channels, and escalate automatically so no alert goes unhandled
    - Spends most of its time handling alerts and needs noise reduction to fight alert fatigue
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion id="advantages" title="What advantages does Flashduty On-call have over other platforms?">
    More flexible and easy-to-use features, more professional service, and more reasonable pricing. Contact us for a procurement guide, or see the comparisons with [PagerDuty](/en/on-call/comparison/vs-pagerduty) and [Opsgenie](/en/on-call/comparison/vs-opsgenie).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing and subscription

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="pricing" title="How is Flashduty On-call priced?">
    We charge by active users and offer three editions: Free, Standard, and Business. See the [pricing page](https://flashcat.cloud/flashduty/price/) for a detailed comparison.

    - Users who use commercial features in a month are active users and must hold a License.
    - Each License corresponds to one account member. For example, with one License only one member can use all features; you can still invite more members, but they can only receive incident notifications.
    - At the end of each monthly cycle, fixed Licenses stay valid and temporary Licenses are released; a deleted member's License is released automatically.

    **License types**: administrators choose a type when allocating a License.

    | Type | Description |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Fixed** | Valid for the purchase period and never preempted; for members who handle incidents and configure the platform |
    | **Temporary** | Released at the end of each cycle; can be taken by allocation or preemption when enough Licenses are available |

    **Members without a License**:

    - Can only passively receive alert messages; escalation rules can still notify them
    - Cannot view the incident list or details, or acknowledge or close incidents; the console shows a permission notice when they sign in
    - Share the tenant's email, SMS, and phone quota with other members
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion id="subscription-expired" title="What happens after my On-call subscription expires?">
    Once the subscription expires, creating is frozen, while your existing data and configuration stay intact:

    - **Alert ingestion stops**: no integration receives new alert events, and no new incidents are created
    - **Historical data stays available**: historical alerts and existing incidents can still be viewed and handled
    - **Creating is frozen**: creating a channel, a schedule, a manual incident, or a war room is disabled
    - **On-page notices**: the Alert List, the Incident List, and the sidebar show a persistent subscription-expired notice
    - **Renewal restores everything**: alert ingestion resumes automatically after renewal — no reconfiguration needed
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

    Flashduty does not validate the domain of bot webhook URLs, so you can:

    1. Select any bot type (for example, Dingtalk Bot) in the escalation rule
    2. Set the webhook URL to your own server endpoint
    3. Parse the request body on your server using that IM platform's message format

    Flashduty sends alert notifications to your server in that platform's message format, and you can process and forward them to any channel.

## Not receiving notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="no-voice-call" title="Why can't I receive voice notifications?">
    Voice calls depend heavily on signal and phone settings. If you keep missing them, check in order:

    1. For numbers registered outside Mainland China, make sure your region is [supported](#voice-numbers)
    2. Check your phone's blocklist or call log for blocked calls (usually landlines starting with 010 or 021); if found, unblock and allowlist them
    3. If the phone shows no blocking, your carrier may have a blocking service enabled:
       - **China Mobile**: check through the WeChat official account "中国移动高频骚扰电话防护"
       - **China Telecom**: check through the official account "天翼防骚扰"
       - **China Unicom**: check through the official account "沃助理", or call customer service
    4. If your number has been ported between carriers, check blocking with each carrier
    5. Restart the phone, reinsert the SIM, or try the SIM in another phone to rule out device or SIM issues

    If you still can't find the cause, contact us.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion id="no-email" title="Why can't I receive email notifications?">
    1. Check your spam folder; if the email is there, move it out
    2. Check whether your email client has auto-delete rules; if so, change them
    3. Ask your company's email administrator whether the server blocks the messages (enterprise mail such as Gmail limits heavy senders); if so, allowlist the sender

    If you still can't find the cause, contact us.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security and stability

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion id="stability" title="How does Flashduty On-call ensure its own stability?">
    **SLA commitment**:

    - **Availability**: core functionality is available 99.95% of the time
    - **Delivery**: 99.95% of alerts are delivered within 5 minutes of triggering

    **How we meet it**:

    1. **Multi-datacenter active-active**: infrastructure spans multiple data centers, and all stateful components run active-active
    2. **Asynchronous processing**: alerts enter an async pipeline immediately, with retries on errors to reduce the risk of loss
    3. **Redundant notification**: important alerts can loop through multiple channels to ensure delivery
    4. **Global acceleration**: enabled for api.flashcat.cloud to keep reporting stable worldwide
    5. **Continuous monitoring**: comprehensive system metrics and regular stress tests to catch problems early
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion id="data-security" title="How does Flashduty On-call ensure data security?">
    1. Full-stack HTTPS for secure transmission
    2. Flashduty encrypts sensitive information at rest and anonymizes logs before writing them
    3. MFA required for important data changes, with operation audit
    4. Regular third-party security certification audits recognized in China and internationally (ISO27001, ISO9001)
    5. NDAs available for customers who need them
    6. Privacy: [View Privacy Policy](/en/compliance/data-security)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion id="private-deployment" title="Does Flashduty On-call support private deployment?">
    Yes. Flashduty On-call offers a private deployment nearly identical to the SaaS service; because it costs more to maintain, its pricing differs from SaaS. Unless you need it, we recommend the cloud service. Contact us if you need the private version.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
