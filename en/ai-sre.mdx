## What is AI SRE?

Flashduty AI SRE is a conversational, autonomous SRE Agent platform. You give instructions in natural language. It plans the steps, queries metrics and logs, runs commands, calls external tools (MCP), and delegates subtasks when needed. It ends with a conclusion backed by its investigation.

When an incident triggers or a war room opens, start a session in one click, and the Agent starts with the incident context. Chat with it in the console, or @-mention it in your IM group (Slack / Feishu / DingTalk / WeCom).

## Activation and billing

AI SRE is available on every plan, must be activated separately in the console, and is billed on actual usage in credits. It does not require an On-call subscription or a member license. See [Activation and billing](/en/ai-sre/overview#activation-and-billing).
